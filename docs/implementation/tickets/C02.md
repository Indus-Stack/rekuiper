# C02 — Freeze capabilities and plan requirements

Baseline `004f095e90dda5f95f075cbcd835b09dcef3a511`.

## Interfaces

- `crates/rekuiper-core/src/contracts/capability.rs` defines `CapabilityDescriptor`, its supported capability set, capacity limits, and separate qualification level. Claims cover input and result modes, ordering scope, replay, acknowledgements, durability, transactions, snapshot, restore, migration, and determinism. A missing descriptor, capability, or limit provides no support. Qualification defaults to unqualified.
- `CapabilityRequirements` describes the explicit capability set, minimum capacity limits, and minimum qualification for a component. `CapabilityComponent` identifies the source, operator, action, or connection by its stable logical ID.
- `check_plan_requirements` checks all of a plan's component requirements and reports every failing component and reason. It orders components by role and then ID, with capability failures first, capacity limits next, and qualification last. Modes and ordering scopes are checked as explicit declarations. Qualification alone cannot supply a missing support claim.
- `crates/rekuiper-core/src/contracts/mod.rs` exports the contract through the core crate.

## Compatibility and follow-up

C02 adds a pure checker. The existing execution and validation entry points continue to use their existing contracts. The caller derives component requirements from its plan, including durable requirements and the relationship between producer output and consumer input modes. Requirements are empty for the legacy lightweight case.
